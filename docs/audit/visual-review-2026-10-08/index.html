IndiFit Visual Review

The bones are good: one typeface, a semantic token system, a motion policy that honours Reduce Motion, and haptics that fire only after a save. What holds IndiFit back from feeling premium is that almost every screen is built from the same flat bordered box, the brand green fails as a text colour in dark mode, and the moments that should feel like a reward (seeing your target, building a thali, finishing a workout, unlocking a badge) look like forms. Fix contrast and consistency first. Then put the effort into five signature moments instead of decorating every screen.

F1

Brand green fails as text in dark mode

S

Dark action (#087F5B) is used for text, outlined buttons and selected chip labels. It measures 2.48:1 on selected, 3.57:1 on cards and 3.96:1 on the page; AA needs 4.5:1. Light mode passes (4.83:1).

Where: plan filters, calendar Day/Week/Month and selected day, player exercise chips, "Quick workout", "Finish partially…", "View workout history", Progress "This Week", the date pill, "Set size", "Customize a copy".

Fix: add an actionForeground token (dark #34D399, light #0F766E) to B05SemanticColors and use it for text, outlined and chip-label roles in app_theme.dart. Keep #087F5B for filled buttons, where white text measures 5.0:1. Refresh goldens.

F2

The thali plate leaks an internal unit and overflows

S

Under the rice it reads "1 householdReference · 130 kcal". circular_thali_plate.dart lines 336 and 456 print quantity.unit.name instead of calling the existing thaliQuantityLabel(). Plate text is hard-coded at 8.5–11 pt, spills out of the katoris, and the "Add" katori overlaps the rim.

Fix: use thaliQuantityLabel(); move names to a legend under the plate (see the concept below).

F3

Dark launch screen, then a light app

XS

The launch storyboard is navy (#060A12). With the phone in light mode the app opens light, so every cold start flashes dark to white.

Fix: give the storyboard a named colour with Any and Dark appearances, or make dark the first-run default (decision 1).

F4

"Use this plan" is silent, then offers to switch to itself

S

Audit item R-08 is still present. The first tap adopts the plan with no visible change; the second asks "Switch from Beginner — 3-Day Full Body to Beginner — 3-Day Full Body?"

Fix: a confirmation, return to Training, and a "Current plan" state on the overview.

F5

Search results move under your finger

S

When the online "Continental Paneer" match arrived, it was inserted at the top and every row shifted down. My tap on "Add" for Paneer Bhurji missed.

Fix: keep local results in place and append online matches below a "More matches" divider.

F6

The same number, formatted three ways

S

"1,440 kg" on the summary, "1440.0 kg" in Progress; "2,546 kcal" and "2543 kcal/day"; "3.8 g" and "3.8g"; onboarding shows "112.0 g" and "365.4 g".

Fix: route every user-facing number through consumer_number_label.dart; add a lint-style test for raw toStringAsFixed in widgets.

F7

Headers that repeat or disagree

S

Log food stacks "Log breakfast · Today" over "Add to Breakfast · Add breakfast". The Food tab uses a small title while Training and Progress use large ones. Training uses ALL CAPS section labels; Today and Food use title case. In Progress, cards are different widths.

Fix: one page-title style per tab, one section-header style, full-width cards.

F8

Fat looks like water

XS

Fat uses a blue water-drop icon, the same symbol and colour as Hydration a few rows below. Your Reference Guide § 10 assigns fat red or coral.

Fix: fixed macro colours app-wide (see Direction).

Direction

What premium means for IndiFit

These follow your Reference Guide § 10 (premium, athletic, calm during workouts, not monochrome) and make it concrete.

Dark first, with depth

Dark is already the stronger look. Replace "border on everything" with three surface levels: page, section with a faint top highlight, and raised with a soft gradient and shadow.

Continuous corners, larger radii

Today's 8/10/12 px rounded rectangles read as Material defaults. Squircles at 16 (rows), 22 (cards) and 28 (sheets) read as iOS-native and expensive.

Numbers are the hero

One big number per screen, in Outfit with tabular figures so timers and counters don't jitter. Everything else steps down.

Colour means one thing

Protein teal, carbs amber, fat coral, fibre violet, water blue, everywhere. Brand green is for actions and success only.

One reward moment per flow

Spring physics, not linear fades. Every animation pairs with a haptic you already have and is skipped under Reduce Motion, which B05MotionPolicy already supports.

Specifically Indian

Steel thali and katori, FSSAI veg and non-veg marks on foods, Hinglish-friendly copy. Competitors cannot copy this, so it is where the visual budget should go first.

Signature moments

Five places to spend the effort

Each concept below is drawn in HTML to show the direction. All of them can be built in Flutter with CustomPainter, built-in fragment shaders and pre-rendered art. None needs a 3D engine; "3D" here means depth, light and tilt.

A · Thali builder

A steel thali you fill

Now: a small plate of identical dark circles with 8.5 pt labels that overflow.

Concept: a top-down steel thali seen at a slight angle. Each katori fills with the dish's colour (dal yellow, sabzi green, curd white), rice sits as a mound and rotis stack. The rim is a macro ring that grows as you add. New dishes drop in on a spring with a light haptic. Names move to a legend under the plate, so text keeps the user's text size.

Build with: CustomPainter plus one fragment shader for brushed steel; optional sensors_plus tilt. Food colours come from each dish's category, so no per-dish art is needed.

Now: a thin strip with "Rest 1:17" and three small buttons, above a dense set table.

Concept: after "Log set", the strip expands into a calm takeover: a large ring counting down, what comes next ("Set 2 · 60 kg × 8"), and −15 / +30 / Skip as thumb-sized targets. D · Badges

Medals with weight

Now: a Material icon inside a tinted circle, in the unlock sheet and on the badges grid.

Concept: each badge is a pre-rendered 3D medal (bronze, silver, gold tiers). It tilts with the phone, a light sheen crosses it on unlock, and locked badges are matte silhouettes. Drag the medal here to see the tilt.

Build order

Order and gates

One PR per step, CI green, goldens refreshed, a Reduce Motion test for anything that animates, and a feel check by you on a real iPhone (the simulator can't judge haptics or 120 Hz motion). Feature freeze applies about 3 days before a store build. My recommendation: V0 before the closed test, V1–V3 if they land before the freeze, the rest from the ready queue.

Your decisions

Five calls before I build past V0

1. Default theme

Dark on first run, or follow the system? Recommend dark first, with the light theme kept as an option. It matches the launch screen and the brand.

2. Animation tool

Rive or Lottie. Recommend Rive: interactive state machines and smaller files. Lottie wins only if you want to buy ready-made animations.

3. Medal and illustration art

Start free with Fluent Emoji 3D (MIT), or commission one consistent set (9 medals plus an onboarding hero). Recommend starting free and commissioning once Plus revenue starts.

4. Exercise images

Approve importing RepDB free-tier images with attribution in About & credits? Recommend yes; it is the single biggest visual gain in Training.

5. First signature moment after V0–V2

Recommend the steel thali (V4); it is what makes IndiFit look like nothing else. The rest takeover (V5) is a close second because lifters see it 15+ times a session.
