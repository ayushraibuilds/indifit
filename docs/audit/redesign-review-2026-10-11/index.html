IndiFit Redesign Review

The redesign delivered what the 8 Oct review asked for, screen by screen. The steel thali, rest takeover, 3D medals and onboarding reveal look right, and the contrast and token work makes every tab feel like one app. Where it falls short of the feel is timing. Most reward moments either play while you can't see them or fight another moment for the same second. Four of the six open PRs are also not ready to merge (red CI or conflicts).

Verdict

Right look, wrong timing

LandsLook and system

Readable brand green, squircles, depth instead of borders, one macro colour map across Today, thali, onboarding and summary. Dark-first feels premium. Light mode holds up with soft shadows.

LandsFour signature screens

Steel thali art, rest takeover, Fluent 3D medals, and the welcome → ruler → target reveal. Each matches its concept closely.

MissesOne reward per flow

The Today chip plays off-screen. The medal sheet covers the summary hero. The exercise-done beat sits under the rest takeover. A permission sheet covers the first takeover.

Moments that miss

Five reward moments, and when you actually see them

The direction's rule was "one reward moment per flow". Each moment works when tested alone. Together, in a real session, they cancel each other out.

M-1

The Today log chip plays off-screen

high

Today lives in an IndexedStack (main_navigation_scaffold.dart:78). TodayNutritionHero.didUpdateWidget starts the chip and the ring count-up as soon as the record stream changes, and that happens while you're on the Food tab. On the normal path (Today → Log food → Food tab → Add → back to Today), both animations finish before you return. I logged poha three times and never saw a chip. The ring was already at 690.

Fix: queue the chip and the previous totals, and play them when Today is the visible tab and route (tab index plus TickerMode or a route-aware check). Add a test that logs while Today is hidden and then switches tabs.

M-2

The medal sheet covers the summary hero

high

After "Complete workout", the summary's tick and hero number start, and within about a second "2 Milestones Reached!" slides up over them with confetti. The recording catches the hero at 1,159 kg mid count-up, then it's behind the sheet. V6 and V7 are both good. Played together, neither lands. On a first workout this happens every time, because First Sweat always unlocks.

Fix: let the summary finish first (tick → count-up → pill). Then show unlocks as a row on the summary that opens the sheet on tap, or open the sheet only after a pause. Either way, one celebration per workout (TP-6).

M-3

The exercise-done beat sits under the rest takeover

medium

When the last Leg Press set is logged, the rest takeover opens for the next exercise ("Up next: Incline Dumbbell Bench Press") at the same time. "Leg Press done · 3 × 8 · 60 kg" shows as a small card below it, with Leg Press's old set rows still behind. The timer holds at 1:30 for about 1.5 s, and the "Up next" line flips from "8–12 reps" to "8 reps".

Fix: sequence them. Play the beat first, alone (1 s, tap to skip), then open the takeover. Or put "Leg Press done" in the takeover header for its first second.

M-4

The alert permission sheet covers the first rest takeover

medium

The first time you log a set, "Get an alert when rest ends" slides up over the new takeover. The first impression of the screen you see 15+ times a session is half a ring behind a permission request.

Fix: ask from the takeover itself (a small "Alert me when rest ends" row), or ask after the first rest ends, when the value is obvious.

M-5

You never see the whole thali

high

The name field, katori banner, quick-start chips and the plate/list toggle stay pinned at the top (about 350 pt). The partial-data notice, totals and Log Thali stay pinned at the bottom. That leaves about 300 pt of scroll for a 340 pt plate. Either the top or the bottom katori is always cut off, and the legend is only visible once the plate is half gone. The signature screen never shows itself whole.

Also on this screen: loading a preset blanks the whole screen behind a spinner for about 3 s (debug build). The katori banner then appears late and pushes the plate down about 40 pt. The empty state is an icon card, not the empty steel plate. The macro badges read "~22.1g", "~104.5g".

Fix: make name, banner and quick start part of the scroll view so they scroll away. Keep only the totals bar pinned. Check that the plate and legend fit on first open at 390 pt and 430 pt widths.

Against the direction

The six principles from 8 Oct

Dark first, with depth met

Dark is the first-run theme, sections have a top highlight, and hero cards are raised. Light uses shadows, as decided.

Continuous corners met

Squircles on surfaces, sheets and buttons. 119 direct BorderRadius.circular sites remain to migrate as files are touched, which the plan allows.

Numbers are the hero mostly

Today ring, onboarding target, rest timer and summary hero all lead with one big tabular number. Progress is still numbers in boxes, and formats still vary (O-1).

Colour means one thing mostly

Protein teal, carbs amber, fat orange with the half-drop, fibre violet, everywhere they're coloured. The Food diary macro row and thali badges don't use the map. Breakfast orange sits next to fat orange.

One reward moment per flow not met

The moments exist but overlap or play unseen (M-1 to M-4). This is the main gap between what was built and the feel we were after.

Specifically Indian mostly

The steel thali carries this, and the welcome tagline names Indian meals. FSSAI veg and non-veg marks on foods, from the same review, weren't planned and aren't there.

Outside the plan but rated weak or plain on 8 Oct and still unchanged: Progress (no charts, mixed card styles), the Today setup card (half the first screen, three buttons), plan cover art, and the Settings profile header.

What to do next

Order and gates

  1. Choreograph the moments

    M-1 to M-4 in one PR on top of the combined branch, because they cross V3, V5, V6 and V7. Gate: a recording of one real session (log food, return to Today, three sets, finish) where each moment plays once, alone and in view.

  2. Give the thali its screen

    M-5 in #91: scrollable header, plate and legend visible on open, banner reserved from the first frame, no full-screen spinner on preset. Gate: simulator shots at 320, 390 and 430 pt showing the whole plate.

  3. Make the PRs mergeable

    Tooltip-test regex, the #91 and #97 conflicts, golden refresh on #92–#94, tracker update. Gate: CI green on all six.

  4. Consistency sweep

    O-1 to O-4: one duration and date formatter, sentence case on Progress, thali and player, full-width cards, the current-set icon, picture placeholders. Add the toStringAsFixed lint test from V0.

  5. Feel check on a real iPhone

    Haptics, 120 Hz, thali and medal tilt, and frame rate on preset load can't be judged on the simulator. This is your gate before merging V4 and V5.

Your decisions

Decided, 11 Oct

1. End of workout: the summary hero leads

Tick, count-up and week pill play first. Unlocks show as a "2 badges unlocked" row on the summary that opens the medal sheet on tap. No automatic sheet.

2. Rest alerts: asked from inside the takeover

A quiet "Alert me when rest ends" row in the takeover on the first rest. The system prompt appears only when it's tapped.

3. Progress gets a short V9

One stat component and a weekly volume chart, after the five steps above. TP-9 (muscle heatmap) stays in the training plan.

Checked on a scratch branch that merges main (d34aa31) with #91, #92, #93, #94 and #97 (which includes #96). Debug build on the iPhone 17 simulator (iOS 26.5), fresh install, dark then light. Motion was checked from screen recordings, decimated to frames. Code facts come from the merged tree.

Not covered: real-device haptics, 120 Hz and tilt; Dynamic Type at large sizes; the thali and player in light mode; Settings; Android; Reduce Motion on device (the PRs carry tests for it).
