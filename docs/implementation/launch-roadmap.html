PR-A Food pack v1, thali works Merged

The catalogue became bundled pack v1 with schema v24 and a one-transaction importer, leaving one nutrition-fact source. Thalis and recipes compute kcal, unknown calories block logging, the chip passes meal and date, and presets use exact foods. (CAT-1…4)

#61

PR-B Store honesty Merged

No fake cloud card. Privacy policy, support and version in Settings, and a policy link in the AI consent sheet. Device IDs in the privacy manifest. "Never retained" and "±30%" are gone, plain words replace jargon, and a copy-lint test guards it.

#62

PR-C Daily-loop correctness Merged

The portion stepper moves in ½ steps (¼ at or below 1, 10 g for weights). The Today ring shows calories against target. The onboarding page no longer resets when the keyboard closes.

#63

PR-D Rest alerts Merged

The first rest explains alerts, then asks for notification permission. Android falls back to an inexact alarm and offers "Allow precise rest alerts" once. A sticky rest bar keeps time left, −15, +30 and Skip on screen.

#65

PR-E Catalogue pipeline and pack v2 Merged

tool/catalog/ builds and validates every pack in CI. Pack v2 fixes 63 portion sizes, retires 37 templated variants (75 in all), adds gram weights to 316 foods and 392 Hinglish aliases, and gives the honest count: 243 dishes. (CAT-5, CAT-6)

#72

PR-F AI portions Merged

"1 bowl dal" logs 2 katori, "1 plate biryani" 2 katori, "150 g rice" 1 katori and "1 katori curd" 150 g. Plain "dal" is Toor Dal Tadka, and size variants no longer crowd the choices. A calibrated katori wins. (CAT-12)

TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1 in every configuration, portrait-only Info.plist, and portrait locked at boot.

At most one manifest check a day, Wi-Fi by default and never in Offline Mode. Packs are checksummed, gated on the real build number and imported in one transaction. Settings → Food database shows the version, size and sources. (CAT-7)
