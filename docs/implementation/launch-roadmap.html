IndiFit Launch Roadmap

The final audit on 5 Oct drove the app on a simulator and probed the real food catalogue. It found that the thali, IndiFit's headline feature, records 0 kcal for real foods and saves to the wrong meal and day. It also found a cloud backup screen for a service that doesn't exist. Two fix batches (PR-A and PR-B) come before the closed test. On 6 Oct, food data moved to catalogue packs: downloaded and checked, then stored and searched on the phone. PR-A now ships the first pack. Two dates drive the plan: Play's 14-day closed test, and Firebase making App Check mandatory for AI on 2 Nov 2026.

What the final audit found (details in docs/audit/FINAL_LAUNCH_AUDIT_2026-10-05.md):

  1. Every thali logs 0 kcal and still says "Thali logged successfully!" Thali and recipe maths read a fact table that the 535 bundled foods aren't in. Seen live, and confirmed by probe tests for every preset.
  2. A thali opened from any meal or day goes to today's Lunch. The Indian Thali chip passes no meal or date.
  3. Presets pick the wrong foods: "egg" becomes Baingan Bharta (eggplant), "curd" becomes a whole Home Thali.
  4. "Encrypted cloud backup · Back up now" is shown, though v1 has no cloud, and it blames the user's connection.
  5. indifit.app is a parked page with no mail: no privacy policy URL, and privacy@indifit.app bounces. The app also has no in-app privacy policy link.
  6. AI is on in release builds, but attestation isn't registered, and enforcement becomes mandatory on 2 Nov.

How to read this page. It gives order and gates, not calendar dates, because agents write the code and speed isn't the limit. Only a few outside clocks are fixed: Play's 14-day closed test, App Check becoming mandatory on 2 Nov, Apple enrolment and review, and the INDB reply. Hard rules:

  • no invented numbers, and never paywall something that was free;
  • CI green before merge, with one PR per topic;
  • freeze bigger changes about 3 days before a store build.

Everything else can move earlier.

Do these first.

1. Approve PR-A (thali) and PR-B (store honesty). Testers will open the thali first.  2. Host the privacy policy and set up email forwarding for indifit.app.  3. Create the Play Console app and recruit 12 testers, enrol in the Apple Developer Program, and generate the upload keystore.  4. Register Play Integrity and App Attest, and verify AI on store-signed builds about a week before 2 Nov.  5. Email the INDB authors for permission to use their data. It costs nothing, and the answer decides how the catalogue grows.

Decided 6 Oct: food data is online-sourced, locally served. Training stays fully offline. Food data ships as versioned packs: a manifest plus small gzip files on Firebase Hosting. The app checks for updates at most once a day, verifies each pack and imports it in one step. Search, logging and the thali then read one fact source on the phone. There is no search server, and the expected cost at launch scale is about ₹0. Plan and tracker (CAT-1 … CAT-13): docs/implementation/NUTRITION_CATALOGUE_PACKS_PLAN.md.

Decided 6 Oct: training gets a reward loop, in v1. Lifters see a "New best" when a set beats every earlier one. It is computed only from logged sets, never estimated. Training gets a weekly goal ("2 of 3 this week · 4 weeks in a row") instead of a daily streak, so rest days never break anything. The workout summary becomes the payoff, with one celebration and proper haptics. These are PR-L, M and N; they ship to testers as soon as they merge. Plan and tracker (TP-1 … TP-14): docs/implementation/TRAINING_PROGRESS_PREMIUM_PLAN.md.

PR-A Food pack v1, thali works P0

Today's catalogue becomes bundled pack v1: a pack format, schema v24 and an importer that writes every food's facts in one transaction. That leaves one nutrition-fact source, so thalis and recipes compute kcal. Logging is refused when calories are unknown. The thali chip passes meal and date. Presets use exact foods and their own portions. The summary shows failures instead of "Calculating…". Adds a real-catalogue test harness. (CAT-1…4)

Size: L · Proof today: probe tests show NULL energy for all 4 presets

PR-B Store honesty P0

Hide the cloud-backup card. Privacy policy, support and version rows in Settings, plus a link in the consent sheet. Device IDs added to the privacy labels. Drop "never retained" and "±30% variance". Plain words instead of "Canonical nutrition snapshot", "bundled" and "Log Snapshot (<10s)", with a copy-lint test.

Size: M

PR-C Daily-loop correctness P1

The portion stepper adds ½ katori instead of compounding (it shows "3.0517578125 katori" today). The Today ring shows calories against target, not a macro pie. Fixes the onboarding page desync and adds a Done key on number pads.

Size: M

PR-D Rest alerts P1

Ask for notification permission on the first rest (iOS never asks today). Android falls back to an inexact alarm when exact alarms are denied. A sticky rest bar keeps the countdown on screen while logging.

Size: M

PR-E Catalogue pipeline and data v2 P1

A build pipeline in tool/catalog/ with a validator that CI runs on every pack. Its first overlay fixes 29 "Double / Small bowl" rows that say "1 katori" and 6 rows storing grams as katori. It also retires nonsense variants such as "Idli … with extra cheese". Adds gram weights per serving and an honest store count (about 260 dishes). The result is pack v2. (CAT-5, CAT-6)

Size: M · After: PR-A

PR-F AI portions, then eval P1

Bowl, plate and gram amounts convert using the packs' gram weights. "Katori dal" auto-matches toor dal. Templated variants leave the choices. Then the paid eval rerun with label and meal photos. (CAT-12)

Size: M · After: PR-E · Needs: your photos and App Check token

PR-H Food-data updates P1

The app checks a small manifest at most once a day (Wi-Fi by default, never in Offline Mode), downloads only new packs, verifies their checksums and imports them. Settings → Food database shows the version and a "Check now" button. New foods arrive without an app release. (CAT-7)

Size: M · After: PR-A · Needs: packs on Firebase Hosting (CAT-8)

PR-G iPhone-only, portrait P1

The app ships to iPad and landscape today, untested, and App Review tests on iPad.

Size: S

PR-L New bests v1

When a logged set beats every earlier comparable set, the row shows "New best" with a success haptic. There are two kinds: heaviest weight, and most reps at that weight or more. Warm-ups, assisted, tempo and drop sets are left out, and the first session is the baseline. Also shown on the summary, the share card and an exercise's "Best ever" card. Derived from logged sets only: no estimates, nothing stored. (TP-1…3)

Size: M–L · Order: L, then M, then N

PR-M Weekly training goal v1

"2 of 3 this week · 4 weeks in a row" on Training, Progress and the summary. The goal is the plan's scheduled days, or your own (default 3). A week in progress never breaks the streak, and changing the goal never rewrites past weeks. The daily streak leaves training screens and stays on Today as "days logged". (TP-4, TP-5)

Size: M · After: nothing; can run beside L

PR-N Summary payoff, motion, haptics v1

The summary leads with "1,440 kg lifted", then new bests, then a "vs last time" line per exercise and "2 of 3 this week". There is one celebration per workout, using the existing confetti. Set rows tick and numbers count up using the app's existing motion timings, and everything is instant with reduce motion. New haptics: success (best, week goal) and rest end. "External volume" becomes "Total lifted". (TP-6, TP-7)

Size: M · After: L and M

PR-T Supporter tip jar v1

"Support IndiFit" in Settings with three small one-off purchases (for example ₹49, ₹99 and ₹199) and a thank-you screen. Nothing is unlocked, so nothing free ever becomes paid. It starts revenue and shows who the paying fans are before Plus arrives, whenever you choose to launch it.

Size: S–M · Needs: your store agreements and products
